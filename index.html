Selected work

GraphRAG · Agents

Ontology-guided service impact analysis

Designed a manager–planner–executor agent system for telecom inventory reasoning. The approach uses a knowledge graph over APIs and parameters to guide retrieval, validate tool sequences, and improve grounded multi-hop answers over vector-only retrieval.

Small Models · RL

Next-best-action reasoning with small language models

Exploring parameter-efficient fine-tuning and group-relative policy optimization for selecting the next node in a constrained reasoning path—reducing latency while retaining structured, auditable decisions.

Forecasting · ML

Forecasting and predictive monitoring

Built machine-learning pipelines for forecasting, anomaly detection, and operational decision support at large scale, with evaluation and monitoring designed for deployment rather than one-off model accuracy.

About

I hold an M.S. in Data Science from Columbia University and work in applied machine learning. I am preparing for doctoral research in computer science, with the long-term goal of advancing reliable AI for healthcare and other knowledge-intensive domains.

I am especially interested in the question: Can graph-constrained symbolic reasoning at inference time make an AI system’s generated path causally faithful and traceable?
