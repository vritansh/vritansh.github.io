About me

I hail from Kullu, a hill town in Himachal Pradesh. I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at SRM Institute of Science and Technology in South India, then spent several years building applied data and AI systems across industry.

I later pursued an M.S. in Data Science at Columbia University, where I deepened my foundation in machine learning, deep learning for computer vision and NLP, statistical inference, and large-scale AI systems. My experience includes data-science internships and applied roles spanning healthcare, telecom, and enterprise AI.

I now work on practical ML and AI systems while preparing for doctoral research in computer science. My long-term aim is to advance reliable, explainable AI that can earn trust in healthcare and other high-impact settings.

Research interests

Research question: Can language models reason reliably over adapted domain knowledge when their generation is constrained and verified through neuro-symbolic structure? I am particularly interested in whether graph-constrained inference can make a model’s reasoning path more causally faithful, inspectable, and robust.

Neuro-symbolic AI

Learning with structure

Bridging neural representations with symbolic constraints, ontologies, and explicit rules for dependable reasoning.

Agents & graphs

Grounded multi-step agents

Using knowledge graphs and tool schemas to guide planning, retrieval, action selection, and verification.

Trustworthy AI

Faithfulness & evaluation

Measuring whether an answer and its reasoning are grounded in evidence—not merely plausible-sounding.

Selected projects

GraphRAG · Agents

Ontology-guided service impact analysis

Designed a manager–planner–executor architecture for telecom inventory reasoning. A knowledge graph over APIs and parameters guides retrieval, validates tool sequences, and supports traceable multi-hop decisions beyond vector-only RAG.

Small models · RL

Next-best-action reasoning
