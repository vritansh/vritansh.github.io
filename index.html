Research interests

My central question is: can LLMs reason reliably over domain-adapted knowledge when their inference is guided and verified by neuro-symbolic structure? I am particularly interested in graph-constrained reasoning, faithful multi-hop retrieval, small-language-model post-training, multi-agent systems, and explainable AI.

  • Neuro-symbolic AI, knowledge graphs, and causal or multi-hop reasoning
  • LLM agents, tool use, planning, and trustworthy evaluation
  • Deep learning, multimodal learning, and AI for healthcare
