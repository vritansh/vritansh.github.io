Research interests

I am interested in reliable and useful machine-learning systems, particularly where learning, reasoning, and decision-making must work under real constraints.

  • How can deep reinforcement learning improve decisions in structured, high-stakes environments?
  • How can post-training make language models more capable, efficient, and dependable?
  • How can neural models reason over knowledge graphs and symbolic constraints?
  • How can we identify and mitigate bias while improving fairness and interpretability in ML systems?
  • How can medical AI quantify uncertainty before supporting decisions in high-risk settings?
  • How can applied mathematics, optimization, and graph algorithms improve modern AI systems?
